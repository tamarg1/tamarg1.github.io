About

I am an AI/Machine Learning leader and researcher specializing in computer vision, deep learning, and responsible generative architectures. My work spans foundational representation research to scalable industrial deployment, with a focus on feature disentanglement, multimodal representation, content safety, and algorithmic fairness[cite: 1].

Research Focus & Expertise

Foundational Machine Learning Models & Architectures

Researching transformer architectures, multimodal foundation frameworks, continual learning, and fine-grained unlearning mechanisms in large models[cite: 1].

Content Representation

Designing rich representations for image, video, and audio datasets[cite: 1]. Leveraging temporal context, attention mechanisms, and deep visual embeddings[cite: 1].

Feature Vectors Disentanglement

Investigating latent feature representations to isolate spurious correlations, improve generalization, and enable precise concept removal[cite: 1].

Responsible AI & Safety

Developing algorithmic bias mitigation, fairness metrics, safety guardrails for generative LLMs, and dataset regulatory standards[cite: 1].

Generated Content Detection

Building high-precision detection models for AI-generated media, synthetic text-to-image identification, and leading workshop tracks on DeepFake detection[cite: 1].

Recommendation Systems

Architecting production multimodal retrieval and recommendation engines driven by modular deep feature representations[cite: 1].

Computer Vision

Classic and deep learning vision systems covering multi-target tracking, image registration, automated event classification, and multi-scale temporal context[cite: 1].
